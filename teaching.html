Teaching

Teaching philosophy

My goal in the classroom is to make my students more informed citizens, thinkers, and researchers. Alongside course content, I try to build broadly applicable skills like strategic thinking and clear exposition. I favor memorable and meaningful social interaction over technology-heavy approaches—discussion, debate, and applied problem-solving over slides.
