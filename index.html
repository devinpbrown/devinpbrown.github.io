Hello!

I am a PhD Candidate and Hagler Graduate Fellow at Texas A&M University, Bush School of Government and Public Service. I expect to graduate in the spring of 2027. My research focuses broadly on non-state involvement in state security. My dissertation is entitled Dangerous Delegation? States’ Motivations to Outsource Security and focuses on state’s incentives to collaborate with pro-government militias and UN peacekeepers. My other research focuses on private firm involvement in US border security.

I earned my MA in International Relations from Koç University in Istanbul, Turkey in 2021. I won a Fulbright ETA to Turkey and taught English at the Social Sciences University of Ankara during the 2018-2019 academic year. I also won a Critical Language Scholarship in 2018 to study Turkish intensively at the Azerbaijan University of Languages, Baku, Azerbaijan.
