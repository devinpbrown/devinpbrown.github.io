Devin P. Brown

PhD Candidate, Political Science, Texas A&M University
Hagler Graduate Fellow, Bush School of Government & Public Service

I am a PhD Candidate in the Department of Political Science at Texas A&M University, where my research focuses on non-state involvement in state security. My dissertation, Dangerous Delegation? States’ Motivations to Outsource Security, examines states’ incentives to collaborate with pro-government militias and UN peacekeepers, advised by Hyeran Jo and Scott Cook. A related line of work, with Beth Simmons, studies private-sector involvement in U.S. border security policy.

I expect to graduate in Spring 2027.
